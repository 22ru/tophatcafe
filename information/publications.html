Mooks and Magazines

Dolly Dolly is published by Graphic-sha. Last issue (39) was published in 2019.

Dollybird is published by Hobby Japan. Some dolls have been exclusively sold through this magazine. This magazine is still publishing.

Dolly Japan is also published by Hobby Japan. There are 5 volumes (0-4). Last issue published in 2015.

U-noa Freak is also published by Hobby Japan. It includes fanmade patterns and tutorials for Unoa dolls. Three volumes were published.

Western magazines that featured BJD included Haute Doll, Dolls, Dollreader, and Fashion Doll Quarterly (FDQ). The main focus was rarely BJD, but they did appear. Both magazines have ceased publication.

Doujinshi

Voleur de Rose

Doujin magazine led by Yun Kouga. Includes patterns, illustrations, and photos. 3 volumes were published, last volume in 2006. Sewing patterns were included for the outfits, but must be enlarged 200%. I have not yet confirmed the patterns being included for the Vol 1 or 2 outfits.

Volume 1: Fashion 🎩

Yun Kouga illustrated the cover, along with a cute boy (Ritsuka?) illustration and 2 short comics. Kyon customized Ritsuka and Soubi are shown.

Images and photos of following outfits:

  • SDB pajama set
  • SD13 long skirt set
  • Unoa corset and panties
  • Unoa girls in baby outfits
  • SD boy shirt, jacket, and shorts
  • SD boy fall coat, pants, scarf

There are illustrations of dolls, a manga of an owner dressing up her two boys in many outfits, manga of a hamster making paper dolls.

Volume 2: Voyage 🎩

Images and photos of following outfits:

  • Unoa girl and boy outfit set in linen
  • Unoss and SD13B set. Unoss wearing a turtleneck, leggings, and overjumper, SD13B wearing pintuck button shirt, scarf, shorts
  • SD girl bustle skirt, tank, and sailor collar
  • Unoss and SD girl kimono coordinates
  • SD13B pajamas and sailor outfit
  • MSDB sailor outfits and blouse and shorts

Full body photos of the Soubi and Ritsuka Kyon customized dolls are shown. They are 22 and 27cm dolls, not BJD.

Manga includes a story of 2 boy dolls interacting with their owner and a "doll essay" manga.

Volume 3: Phantasm 🎩

Scans of the manga have been uploaded by hors!

Images and photos of following outfits:

  • SD Kun as the Ringleader (military style outfit)
  • SD13B(?) Nana and SD Syo as the circus girls
  • SD13B Chris and SD13B F16 in military uniform
  • SD girl beret and retro dress

    • Did not see a pattern for this outfit

  • Unoa Lusis in a kokeshi style
  • bunny stuffie

There is a photo essay and doll sketches by Yun Kouga.

Misc

Japan Ai: A Tall Girl's Adventures in Japan 🎩

An illustrated story about Aimee Major's trip to Glorious Nippon. Select pages are in color. Very cute and enjoyable, but not very long. The final chapter covers her visit to Tenshi no Sato in Kyoto. She has a map of TnS, describes her FCS order, her omukae ceremony, and her dinner with the Shigeta family.
