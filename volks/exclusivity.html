Volks Exclusivity

You may start to wonder

Volks considers all resin BJD and vinyl dolls over a skeleton that are not made by them to be knock offs. This is not limited to recasts.

In the early 2000s, there were lawsuits between Volks and Obitsu. In Case A, Obitsu sued Volks because Volks was telling business partners that Obitsu bodies were knock offs of Volks DD bodiesand Volks would use legal action if the business chose to sell Obitsu dolls. Obitsu won. In Case B, Volks sued Obitsu for selling a similar product. In 2012, Obitsu sued Volks for the DD body being a knock off of Obitsu 50. This case was dismissed.
