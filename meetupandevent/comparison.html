A Comparison of Doll Events

Dolpa in LA

2026 price: $30 pre-reg, $40 at door. $100 for tea party ticket or $150 for tea party ticket plus companion.

Pros

  • Volks doll exclusive
  • very well organized
  • multiple activities outside of vendors and the Sumika shop

Cons

  • Volks doll exclusive
  • the panic of sending a doll home on Labor Day weekend
  • tea party entrance by lottery
  • poor forewarning
  • tea party tickets are expensive
  • it's in LA.

This con is absolutely worth flying to if you win tea party tickets. I'm unsure it's worth flying to if not.

Consider staying Sunday night and leaving on Monday, especially if you have any intention to buy a doll at the con. Although the event ends fully by 4PM, results of the lottery and lottery pick up are finished by around 3:30PM.

Resin Rose

This con feels mostly like a vendor's hall with a few extras added on the side.

Pros

  • Kumukuku and Cocoriang always vend here
  • Scheduled meetups
  • Swap meet

Cons

  • Few places to sit down and hang out outside of meetups
  • A lot of downtime
  • Everyone goes back to their room
  • Bad hotel: mismanaged, little seating, poor layout
  • Clackamas isn't very interesting

This con is good for locals and those who can road trip with their friends. I don't know if I would recommend flying out to it unless you have friends going already because it is hard to make friends at this convention.

If you want to go to this con, consider staying Sunday night so you can leave on Monday. The closing ceremony, which includes the raffle, ends at 8PM on Sunday night and you need to be there (or have a friend) to pick up any raffle prizes.
